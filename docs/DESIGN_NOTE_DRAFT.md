# DESIGN NOTE — DRAFT — Creed to edit in his own words before any sharing

I run a network of youth soccer guide sites. Parents were already asking AI assistants which clubs to look at, and the assistants were answering from whatever they could scrape, without dates and often without credit. I wanted the answer to come from our verified directory, with the date we checked it and a link back.

I didn't want a new vendor, a new bill, or anything that could change our sites. So this runs inside the WordPress sites we already pay for, it only reads what is already published, and it keeps a simple count of how it's used without recording who anyone is.
