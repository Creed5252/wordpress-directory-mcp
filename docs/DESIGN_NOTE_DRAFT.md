# Design note — DRAFT, written from Colin Reed's dictation (2026-11-19), awaiting his read-through

*By Colin Reed, Modern Wealth Model and The Pitch Network.*

## It felt like RSS

I kept seeing MCP servers come up. I knew they had something to do with connectors, but I was still learning what they were. Once I saw one working, it reminded me of an RSS feed, way back in the day. I remember thinking about building one of those back then, and I briefly did, just because it felt early. This is the same feeling.

## Why do it now

It's cheap, it's easy to do, and it's low impact. It seems like something that will get used more and more. If agents start to account for most of the traffic on the internet, it helps to be early. So this runs inside the WordPress sites we already pay for, it only reads what is already published, and it adds no new bill.

## Why open source it

Open sourcing it helps build the reputation of Modern Wealth Model. The commit dates on these builds show how early we were doing this. Two or three years from now, we can point to a long history of education and development in this field.

## What surprised me

Adding the "Connect your AI" pieces to the website pages worked differently on different sites, even though the sites are very similar. Sometimes the cache kept us from seeing the change. I had to refresh several times, and we had to clear the cache before the new version showed up.

## Who it's for

Parents, and anyone researching youth soccer clubs. Also club owners, site owners, and nonprofits. I think there's an obvious reason for them to use tools like this. But not many people know about them yet.

## Why give it away

Getting it out there for free starts the traction. Even if someone just sees it and thinks, "Oh, I can do that. That's a thing. Connect your AI to a website." When I'm going about my own work on the internet, I don't see websites offering to connect my AI assistant. So this is a chance to be early, raise some eyebrows, and maybe get more people interested.

## What I'd like to find out

I'm curious to see how many people actually use it. The server keeps a small count of connections and searches, with no IP addresses, so we'll know.
