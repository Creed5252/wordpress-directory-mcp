# 0004: Serve public fields only, and canonical pages only

Date: 2026-11-19

Club records in the datasets carry internal research notes, legal names and tax IDs used for verification. Only the fields shown publicly on the sites are returned. Pages whose canonical points to another site (a duplicate-content fix made two days earlier) are not served, so agents see each page once, from its home site. Contact emails on listings are not served on any brand.
